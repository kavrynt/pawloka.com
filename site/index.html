Plans

Start without AI. Add it when it pays for itself.

Both plans include the clinic workspace and WhatsApp. The AI plan adds an assistant that understands typed messages and reads documents. Move between plans at any time.

Basic

Tap-first WhatsApp and the full clinic workspace. No AI.

  • The clinic workspace. Appointments, pet records, reminders, payments and the team inbox.
  • WhatsApp by buttons. Clients book, keep or cancel a visit, see vaccinations due and opening hours, or ask for a person, all by tapping.
  • Buttons on every reminder. Keep, rebook or cancel straight from the reminder.
  • Your FAQ, word for word. A typed question that matches one of your FAQ entries gets your answer. Anything else goes to your team.
  • The emergency check. Runs on every message, so urgent cases reach your team straight away.

AI front desk

Everything in Basic, plus an assistant for the messages buttons can't handle.

  • Answers typed questions. From your FAQ, your opening hours and care guides, and it books in the same chat.
  • Reads photos and PDFs. Vaccination cards and invoices go into the record as unverified entries for your team to confirm.
  • Summaries for your team. Every handed-over chat arrives with a short summary at the top.
  • Voice notes as text.Building Transcribed so the assistant and your team can read them.
  • A monthly AI budget you set. When it is used up, chats go to your team instead of the AI, and nothing else stops working.

Pricing is per clinic per month. We're setting it together with our pilot clinics.

A health record that follows the pet, not the paperwork.

Every visit, vaccine and certificate lands in one record. It starts at the breeder, moves home with the owner, and is ready when an insurance claim comes up.

  1. Breeder

    Coming next

Questions

What clinics and sellers ask us first.

Will it give medical advice to my clients?

No. It books, reminds and answers routine questions about your clinic. Possible emergencies go straight to your team, questions about medicines go to a vet, and any reply with a dose that isn't already in the pet's record is held back.

Does Pawloka replace our clinic software?

You don't have to replace anything. Pawloka has its own appointments, pet records, reminders, payments and team inbox, so a clinic without software can start with it. If you already use clinic software, keep it alongside. Connections to the most common systems are on our roadmap, starting with the ones our pilot clinics use.

Do we need the AI plan?

No. On the Basic plan clients book, keep or cancel visits and check vaccinations by tapping buttons, and typed messages go to your team, or get your own FAQ answer when they match it. The AI front desk adds answers to typed questions, reading photos and PDFs, and summaries for your team. You set a monthly AI budget, and when it is used up chats go to your team.

Who at the clinic can do what?

Everyone signs in with their own account, and every change to a record is logged with who made it. With team roles, which we're building now, clinic admins invite the team and manage settings, and only vets can write prescriptions and confirm clinical entries.

What happens when the assistant can't help?

It hands the chat to your team with a short summary, and tells the owner someone from the clinic will reply. If an owner asks for a person, they get one.

Can my clinic sell food and medicines through Pawloka?

Yes. List the diets and medicines you already stock. When a prescription comes up for reorder, owners see your clinic with its price and delivery time, and they can always buy from their own clinic. You keep your margin, and Pawloka charges a small fee per order.

Who can sell on Pawloka?

Clinics and pharmacies with a valid drug licence can sell medicines, and only against a vet's prescription. Pet stores and pet food brands can sell prescribed diets and supplies.

Who can see a pet's record?

The owner, and any clinic, breeder, seller or insurer the owner has given consent to. A seller sees only the prescription it is filling. Access ends when consent ends, and every change is logged.
