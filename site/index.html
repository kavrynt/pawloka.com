Questions

What clinics and sellers ask us first.

Will it give medical advice to my clients?

No. It books, reminds and answers routine questions about your clinic. Possible emergencies go straight to your team, questions about medicines go to a vet, and any reply with a dose that isn't already in the pet's record is held back.

Do we need new clinic software?

No. Clients use WhatsApp as they do today, and your team uses an inbox in the browser. Connections to common clinic management systems are on our roadmap.

What happens when the assistant can't help?

It hands the chat to your team with a short summary, and tells the owner someone from the clinic will reply. If an owner asks for a person, they get one.

Can my clinic sell food and medicines through Pawloka?

Yes. List the diets and medicines you already stock. When a prescription comes up for reorder, owners see your clinic with its price and delivery time, and they can always buy from their own clinic. You keep your margin, and Pawloka charges a small fee per order.

Who can sell on Pawloka?

Clinics and pharmacies with a valid drug licence can sell medicines, and only against a vet's prescription. Pet stores and pet food brands can sell prescribed diets and supplies.

Who can see a pet's record?

The owner, and any clinic, breeder, seller or insurer the owner has given consent to. A seller sees only the prescription it is filling. Access ends when consent ends, and every change is logged.
